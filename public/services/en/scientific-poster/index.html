Scientific Poster Design

A scientific conference poster should help people grasp your study quickly. Clear research-poster design uses a readable hierarchy for the objective, methods, main results and conclusions, with figures that support rather than overwhelm the message.

Request a scientific posterHow it works

What should a scientific research poster include?

Most academic posters organize a research title and authors, brief background or objective, methods, results and conclusions. The layout should give the strongest findings and supporting graphics enough space while remaining legible at the poster's intended viewing distance. Event-specific requirements take priority over generic layouts.

  • A clear research title, authors, affiliations and a concise research objective
  • Scannable sections for methods, primary findings and conclusions
  • Research charts, scientific illustrations and captions with readable labels

What do you need for a conference poster brief?

  1. Gather the final title, author list, affiliations and accepted abstract.
  2. Select the key charts, figures and results that need visual emphasis.
  3. Send the required dimensions, orientation, language and organizer guidelines.

When do you need scientific poster design?

A conference or congress poster is useful for communicating research at scientific meetings and academic exhibitions. It can also support university research presentations when the requested size and audience are specified before design starts.

Which poster requirements should be confirmed?

State the exact size, portrait or landscape orientation, language, institution logos, figures and required file format. For printed posters, share the organizer's resolution and production specifications so the requested output can be assessed.

Scientific poster design questions

Should a conference poster contain the entire paper?
No. A poster usually highlights the research aim, methods, essential results and conclusions. Too much text makes the main findings harder to locate during a conference session.
Can the poster follow an organizer's template?
Provide the template and official formatting rules with your brief. Dimensions, logo placement, font limits and required output formats can then be reviewed before the design is planned.
