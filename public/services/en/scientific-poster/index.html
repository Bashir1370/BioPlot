Scientific Poster Design

A research poster needs to communicate a study quickly in a busy conference setting. Strong hierarchy, well-chosen charts and readable labels make findings easier to follow.

Request a scientific posterHow it works

What goes into a research poster?

Prepare your study title, authorship, abstract, figures and the conference poster dimensions. Decide which result deserves the most visual emphasis and check the organizer's template or branding rules before design.

  • A clear title, research question and concise background
  • Methods, results and conclusions arranged for scanning
  • Charts, scientific figures and captions at readable sizes

Preparing your conference poster brief

  1. Collect the final abstract, headings, author list and institutional details.
  2. Choose the charts, tables and images needed to explain your results.
  3. Provide the required poster dimensions and conference guidelines.
