Scientific Illustration Design

Turn a complex scientific idea into a clear visual explanation. A well-planned illustration can show a mechanism, experiment or biological process without overwhelming your reader.

Request a scientific illustrationHow it works

What can a scientific illustration explain?

Start with your research question, a rough sketch or key references. Identify the biological entities, their relationships and the message the figure should communicate. The design brief can specify labels, journal dimensions and an intended audience.

  • Cellular signaling pathways and molecular interactions
  • Experimental workflows and study designs
  • Biological processes, mechanisms and conceptual models

Preparing your figure brief

  1. Describe the central mechanism or research question.
  2. Send supporting references, a sketch or a list of required elements.
  3. Mention output size, labels and any journal requirements.
