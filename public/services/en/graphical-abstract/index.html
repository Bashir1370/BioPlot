Graphical Abstract Design

A graphical abstract is a compact visual summary of a paper. It should guide readers from the research question through the approach to the main finding or conclusion.

Request a graphical abstractHow it works

What makes a useful graphical abstract?

A useful brief includes your abstract, the most important result and the figure specifications from the journal. Identify which details must remain scientifically exact and which can be simplified for visual clarity.

  • A single, clearly defined take-home message
  • A visual sequence with only the essential methods and results
  • Readable labels and a layout adapted to the intended journal

Preparing your graphical abstract brief

  1. Share the abstract or a concise summary of the study.
  2. Highlight the main result, method and scientific conclusion.
  3. Add the target journal's size and format guidelines.
